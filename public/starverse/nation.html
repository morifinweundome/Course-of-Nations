Write your orders

Build each order from what your nation holds; the basket checks slots, caps and costs as you go. Estimates use this turn's prices; the GM costs every order exactly when the turn is prepared. Your draft is kept in this browser until you send it.

Free-form orders

Treaties, policies, initiatives and anything the builder does not cover. One order per line, beginning with its verb: TREATY, POLICY, INITIATIVE, CONTACT, TRANSFER, CIVIC, SHIFT, REFORM.

Plans

A plan groups orders and covert operations under one objective. Optional; up to five.
